\begin{align*}
z_{2n-1} &:= x_n\\
\end{align*}
and
\begin{align*}
z_{2n} &:= y_n
\end{align*}
for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Show that $(z_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ is convergent if and only if both $(x_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ and $(y_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ are convergent and
\begin{align*}
