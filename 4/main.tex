\section*{Exercise 1}
Let $(b_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a bounded sequence, and $(a_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence with 
\[\lim a_n = 0.\]

Show that 
\[\lim(a_n b_n) = 0.\]

\textbf{Solution:} \\

Since $(b_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ is a bounded sequence, there exists $M > 0$ such that
\[|b_n| \leq M \quad \text{for all } n \in \mathbb{N}\]

Since $\lim a_n = 0$, for any $\epsilon > 0$, there exists $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that
\[|a_n| < \frac{\epsilon}{M} \quad \text{for all } n \geq N\]

Based on the above, for all $n \geq N$, we have:
\[|a_n b_n| = |a_n| \cdot |b_n| \leq |a_n| \cdot M < \frac{\epsilon}{M} \cdot M = \epsilon\]

Therefore, for any $\epsilon > 0$, there exists $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that
\[|a_n b_n| < \epsilon \quad \text{for all } n \geq N\]

By the definition of the limit, we have
\[\lim(a_n b_n) = 0\]

\section*{Exercise 2}
Compute $\lim \frac{n!}{n^n}$, or show that this limit does not exist. \\

\textbf{Solution:} \\

Define $a_n = \frac{n!}{n^n}$ and find the ratio of consecutive terms:

\begin{align*}
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} &= \frac{(n+1)!/(n+1)^{n+1}}{n!/n^n} \\
&= \frac{(n+1) \cdot n! \cdot n^n}{n! \cdot (n+1)^{n+1}} \\
&= \frac{(n+1) \cdot n^n}{(n+1) \cdot (n+1)^n} \\
&= \frac{n^n}{(n+1)^n} \\
&= \left(\frac{n}{n+1}\right)^n < 1 \quad \text{(since $\frac{n}{n+1} < 1$)}
\end{align*}

Since all the terms are nonzero, by Ratio Test for convergence, we have:
\[
\lim \frac{n!}{n^n} = 0
\]
